7. WEEK 2 QUEUE CONTRACT
------------------------
- One public transactional wrapper: create_scan_idempotent, SECURITY DEFINER.
- It calls private helpers internal_create_scan_only and
  internal_enqueue_initial_jobs. Private helpers are not client-callable.
- Initial scan creation inserts exactly four jobs:
  legal, ip, safety, recalls.
- Job identity is (scan_id, check_type).
- anon/authenticated: SELECT on scans and scan_jobs, no direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE.
  scans DELETE is intentionally left for a later decision.
- Queue functions are service_role-only:
  claim_job, heartbeat_job, recover_expired_leases, complete_job, fail_job.
- claim_job increments attempts, uses FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, sets running,
  locks metadata, and issues a lease token. Default max_attempts is 3.
- heartbeat_job extends only a live lease.
- complete_job/fail_job require a live lease token; stale/expired -> SQLSTATE P0409.
- fail_job retries with exponential backoff until max_attempts, then fails.
- recover_expired_leases returns expired jobs to retry_wait with backoff, or fails
  them when the attempt budget is exhausted. It sets error_code LEASE_EXPIRED.
- Error codes: P0400 invalid input, P0401 auth required, P0404 unavailable,
  P0409 stale lease/conflict, P0500 internal.
- SQL/TS contract is validated with Zod in lib/queue/jobs.ts.
